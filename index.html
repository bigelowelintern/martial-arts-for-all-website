Every child deserves a place on the mat.

We believe every child deserves the chance to learn martial arts — not just the ones whose families can afford it. The Martial Arts For All Foundation funds classes and equipment for underserved youth, partnering with local martial arts schools so kids can train alongside their peers in their own community.

We do this because martial arts teaches discipline, respect, and confidence. These are lessons that carry over from the dojo into school and everyday life.

Your donation gives a child the chance to step onto the mat for the first time, and experience the culture and community that make this sport so special.
