About Us

The people behind the mission

Martial Arts For All Foundation was built by people who believe every kid should have a shot at the discipline and confidence martial arts teaches — regardless of what they can afford.

Board of Directors

Guiding the Foundation forward

Meinte Huitema

Founder and Board Member

I've been training karate for several years. Martial arts has made me more disciplined, focused, and confident both on and off the mat. I founded this nonprofit because I believe every child should have that same chance I had to build confidence, develop life skills, and feel supported, regardless of their family's income.

Nathalie Huitema

Board Member

I joined the board because martial arts has shaped my own life and my kids' lives. I've seen firsthand how training builds confidence, discipline, and a sense of belonging. When my youngest came up with the idea for Martial Arts for All, it immediately fit our family values of supporting others and giving back. I'm proud to help make this opportunity available to every child.

Eli Bigelow

Board Member

I've trained and competed in martial arts for four years, and I joined the board to help underprivileged kids experience the same community, camaraderie, and culture I've found in the sport. Beyond being personally rewarding, I believe martial arts builds humility, discipline, and hard work; qualities I've seen make a real difference in young people's development. My hope is to share that culture with as many kids as possible.

Rikke Bitsch-Norhave

Board Member

I'm passionate about volunteering my time, energy, and experience to making ideas happen and bringing people together to create positive change, so joining the board of Martial Arts for All feels like a natural fit.

Everyone should have the chance to reach their potential, regardless of circumstances. Martial arts offer far more than just physical skills—students build confidence, discipline, resilience, focus, and respect that last a lifetime.

Being part of this organization that makes these opportunities accessible is especially meaningful to me.

Martial Arts For All Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit giving Boston-area kids access to martial arts classes and gym memberships.
